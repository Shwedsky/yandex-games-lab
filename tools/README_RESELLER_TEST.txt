RESELLER — LOCAL HUMAN TEST KIT

1. Unpack the ZIP to a normal folder.
2. Double-click START_RESELLER_TEST.cmd.
3. A browser will open automatically with mock rewarded ads enabled.
4. Play normally. The launcher keeps a local server running until you press Enter in its console.

What to test first:
- market card readability and whether good/bad deals feel understandable;
- negotiate -> buy -> inventory -> prepare -> list -> buyer offer -> sell;
- whether profit/loss is obvious after a deal;
- whether the first 10–15 minutes feel too fast, too slow or repetitive;
- rewarded exact-value / refresh / instant-buyer actions;
- day transition and persistence after closing/reopening the page.

mockAds=1 is local-only. It simulates successful Yandex rewarded ads after about 1.4 seconds.
